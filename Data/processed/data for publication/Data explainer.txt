Location Denaturing Statement

To protect potentially sensitive ecological and cultural information, all 
coordinate data in this folder have been generalised to a spatial resolution of 
0.1 decimal degrees (approximately 10 km). This follows best-practice guidance 
from the GBIF Sensitive Species Data Best Practices 
(https://docs.gbif.org/sensitive-species-best-practices/master/en/), and reflects 
a precautionary approach to spatial data sharing.

Datasets:
- apicalis.csv: Primary dataset used in the apicalis SDM analysis. Occurrence 
  coordinates were rounded to 0.1° to minimise location precision while retaining 
  modelling utility.

- Conditor.csv: Primary dataset used in the conditor SDM analysis. Occurrence 
  coordinates were rounded to 0.1° to minimise location precision while retaining 
  modelling utility.

- apicalis_middens.csv and apicalis_true_dates.csv: Supplementary datasets used 
  for exploratory comparisons. These illustrate how temporal structuring 
  (z-axis information) affects model quality and interpretation. They were also 
  denatured to 0.1°.
- Pearson_masterlist_compiled.csv: this is the cleaned and compiled version of Pearson's   master list, which is a work book with multiple sheets. 
	

The raw data folder contains the datasets I have used for this research, includng meta data and refrences. Again, all coordinate data have been generalised to a spatial resolution of 0.1 decimal degrees, unless publicly available.
